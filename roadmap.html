Wuhbu Roadmap

One price, free updates for life — here's what that means in practice. No dates, because a one-parent studio doesn't do well with dates, but this is the order things are coming.

Version 1.0 Launching

Six games for ages 2–4 on iPad:

  • Bubbles — pop, release stars, hunt for the gold one
  • Shapes — drag shapes into their outlines
  • Animals — match the boy and girl cat, dog, cow, pig, frog and duck; each says its name and makes its sound
  • Paint — fat crayons, a rainbow crayon and stickers
  • Piano — eight big keys to tap or slide
  • Fireflies — catch them in the jar

Plus a press-and-hold parent gate, a play timer, sound and music toggles, and no internet needed.

Version 1.1 Next

  • iPhone support — the same app, one purchase, on every iPhone and iPad in the house
  • Small polish based on what our first families tell us

Later Ideas

  • Spanish (and other languages) for the animal names
  • More games — we add, never charge
  • A bigger-kid Wuhbu for ages 4–6 with puzzles, letters and numbers

Have an idea, or a game your little one keeps asking for? Email developer@wuhbu.com. Ideas from real families go to the top of the list.
