Grown-ups' settings

Tap the small gear in the top-right corner of the home screen, then press and hold the button for 3 seconds. There you can turn sounds and music on or off and set a play timer.

Keeping your child inside the app

Use iPadOS Guided Access: Settings ▸ Accessibility ▸ Guided Access ▸ turn on and set a passcode. Open Wuhbu, triple-click the top button, tap Start. Triple-click again and enter your passcode to end.

No sound?

Check the iPad's mute switch or Control Center, then the grown-ups' settings inside Wuhbu.
